# Pilot Questionnaire and Ranking Report

`pilot_condition_v1` remains the authoritative condition questionnaire. It presents 1–7 controls, clear selection state, required-item validation and linkage to current session/assignment/`pilotRunId`. Submit persists answers once and closes the panel.

Questionnaires 1 and 2 lead to a neutral transition. Questionnaire 3 leads to Final Embodiment Ranking. Ranking requires each of Voice Feedback Only, Floating Orb Feedback and Humanoid Assistant Feedback exactly once at ranks 1–3, stores preferred embodiment code/name and reason, then opens Completion.

The controlled manual run submitted all three questionnaires and final ranking through the actual UI. Bundle analysis recovered 9 questionnaire item rows and 3 ranking rows with correct linkage.
